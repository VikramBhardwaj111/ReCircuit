Safety Boundaries

Items Not Accepted in Standard Collection

Keep hazardous household chemicals and non-electronic debris separate from electronics bins. Contact vikram.bhardwaj200710@gmail.com for guidance on bulk or specialized equipment.

Material Type Status Guidance
Leaking wet-cell or automotive acid batteries Not Accepted Requires specialized hazardous-liquid containment facilities.
Medical biohazard or radioactive devices Not Accepted Must be decommissioned through certified clinical waste channels.
Loose household garbage, glass, or paper Not Accepted Sort through standard municipal recycling or waste collection.
